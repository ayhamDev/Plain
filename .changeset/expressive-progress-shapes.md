Add gapped standard and wavy linear/circular progress, the seven-shape expressive LoadingIndicator, and 36 responsive Shape contours with shaped containers, images, buttons and badges. Progress retains its segmented storage API; LinearProgress is its alias. Wave geometry uses pixel-based wavelengths and gaps to avoid stretched strokes on responsive bars.

New components support theme tokens, typed styling slots, native props/refs, tooltips, localized loading text and progress values, RTL, and reduced motion. Direct component exports and optional shape.css keep shape assets and animation engines out of unrelated consumers. Existing Spinner stays available.
